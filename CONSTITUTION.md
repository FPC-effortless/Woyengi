# Woyengi Platform Constitution

## 1. State is reconstructed

Woyengi never treats generated text, retrieved text, model inference, stored documents, or graph edges as equivalent to state. State must be reconstructed from typed historical representations under explicit temporal, provenance, authority, evidence, lifecycle, and permission constraints.

## 2. The kernel is domain-neutral

Domain products may extend Woyengi Platform but may not force domain-specific concepts into the platform kernel.

## 3. History is immutable

Corrections, supersessions, retractions, invalidations, merges, and splits are new records. Materialized views may change; canonical history is append-oriented.

## 4. Time is bitemporal

Important records distinguish when something was valid in the world from when Woyengi recorded it.

## 5. Authority is not confidence

Statistical confidence and governing authority are separate inputs to projection and verification.

## 6. Conflict is inspectable

A selected projection never erases contradictory candidates or the reason for selecting one.

## 7. Indexes are disposable

Search indexes, vector indexes, graph materializations, caches, and current-state projections are rebuildable from canonical records.

## 8. Agent writes are proposals

Read access does not imply write access. Agent-produced observations, claims, events, and actions pass through validation, authority, lifecycle, and permission controls before becoming governing state.
