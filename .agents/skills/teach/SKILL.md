# teach

Create topic learning state under `.agents/learning/<topic>/` unless the user selects another location.

Maintain:
- `MISSION.md` — desired capability and exit criteria;
- `RESOURCES.md` — primary/high-quality sources and why they matter;
- `GLOSSARY.md` — concepts;
- `LESSONS/` — concise explanations and worked examples;
- `LEARNING-RECORD.md` — retrieval attempts, errors, and next review targets.

Teach through retrieval, generation, worked examples, interleaving, and correction rather than passive summaries. Use the strongest sources available and mark uncertainty. Do not conflate familiarity with demonstrated capability.
