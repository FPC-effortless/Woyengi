# to-questionnaire

Use when a blocker cannot be answered from available sources and the missing information belongs to another person/domain expert.

Each question should tie to:
- the decision it affects;
- why current evidence is insufficient;
- the minimum answer needed;
- preferred evidence/examples;
- consequence of no answer.

Avoid asking the recipient for facts the agent could research independently. Store durable questionnaires under `docs/questionnaires/<slug>.md` when they belong to a project workflow.
